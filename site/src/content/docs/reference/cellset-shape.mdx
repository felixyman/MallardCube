A cellset is not just values on axes: Excel reads it structurally, in fixed
positions, and decides from what it finds whether a field can render, expand or
sort. A correct cell count with the wrong member shape shows as an empty field
or a crash on expansion, not as a data error.

## What to check when a field misbehaves

| symptom | first thing to check |
|---|---|
| a field renders empty although the axis has members | the member's `Hierarchy` name and `LNum` — Excel places members by field and level |
| Excel crashes when expanding to a lower level | an unrequested `CHILDREN_CARDINALITY` shifting the positional fields |
| dates render as text, or not at all | the attribute hierarchy's `MEMBER_VALUE` `DATA_TYPE`, and the axis member's `LName` |
| a nested field lists the wrong members | whether the drilldown names the attribute hierarchy or the user hierarchy |
